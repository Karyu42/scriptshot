-- Time-stop dash: pressing Dash freezes the world for a moment, and you
-- dash too.
--
-- How it works:
--   * on_dash runs every time you press the Dash button (when the button is
--     ready again; see `cooldown` below).
--   * world.time_stop(seconds) freezes enemies and their bullets. Your ship
--     and your own shots keep moving, so you can fire into a frozen wall.
--     It lasts at most 3 seconds. Afterwards it needs a rest of 1.5 times as
--     long as it ran (at least 1 second) before it works again.
--     While it rests it just returns false; nothing breaks.
--   * player:dash(angle) is the normal dash: the quick burst and the short
--     moment when bullets cannot hurt you. A time stop on its own does NOT
--     protect you, so we dash as well.
--   * Bosses' white-violet "Null" attacks still move at half speed during a
--     stop. Keep dodging those.

return {
  name = "Time-stop dash",

  -- Seconds before Dash can be pressed again. Try 2 or 3 to match the
  -- time stop's own rest.
  cooldown = 1.5,

  on_dash = function(ctx, dir)
    -- Freeze the world for 1 second. `stopped` is true if it worked,
    -- false if the time stop is still resting (`wait` says for how long).
    -- (`why` is "cooldown" or "active"; you could show `wait` somewhere.)
    local stopped, why, wait = world.time_stop(1.0)

    -- Dash the way you are moving. math.atan turns a direction into an angle.
    player:dash(math.atan(dir.y, dir.x))

    -- Returning nothing counts as a dash, so the Dash cooldown starts.
  end,
}
